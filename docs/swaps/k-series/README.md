# Honda K-Series Swap

## Baseline status

The project treats **K24** as the primary K-series reference because its displacement and torque are better matched to the
944 than a typical K20, while K20 cylinder heads and components remain relevant to high-performance variants.

A 2006 Acura TSX K24A2 provides a useful stock reference: **205 hp and 164 lb-ft** [S014].

## Weight

A detailed 2024 builder weighing exercise on a K24A2 demonstrated why single-number K-engine weights are misleading.
Depending on what is attached, the builder concluded that reasonable K24 figures span roughly **240-320 lb** [S015].
Any 944 comparison must therefore use an explicitly documented installation configuration.

## 2025-2026 944 case study

The Drive documented a budget K24-swapped Porsche 944 beginning in 2025 and reported in September 2026 that the
turbocharged car was finally running and moving under its own power [S016].

The value of this case study is not merely that a K24 can fit. The final report states that:
- the engine itself required teardown and repair, including bent valves,
- the gearbox and hydraulics required additional work,
- essentially every major subsystem needed sorting before the car would move,
- the running turbo combination produced nearly 300 hp at the wheels.

This makes the build evidence for **physical feasibility**, but not evidence that the available conversion path is mature
or bolt-in.

## Why the K24 remains compelling

- all-aluminum four-cylinder architecture
- comparatively low mass
- very large aftermarket
- inexpensive and widely available donor engines
- strong naturally aspirated baseline
- enormous forced-induction development base

## Principal 944 questions

- bellhousing/torque-tube adapter availability and quality
- sump and subframe geometry
- engine angle and hood clearance
- intake and brake-booster clearance
- accessory drive and A/C retention
- clutch hydraulic geometry
- actual 944 swap-ready weight
- repeatability of the 2026 installation

## Current maturity assessment

**Maturity: Emerging.**

The K24 deserves full comparison treatment, but the project should explicitly distinguish "a completed/running example
exists" from "a mature conversion ecosystem exists."
