# GM LS — Current 944 Vendor Ecosystem

## G Force Performance Products

As of the September 2026 sweep, G Force has the clearest fully indexed current online catalog.

### Core kit — GF-LS944-SWAPKIT

Current listed price: **$2,565** [S019].

Includes:
- bellhousing adapter
- GM throwout bearing/slave
- remote bleeder
- custom oil pan/pickup
- 11-in sprung Porsche-spline clutch disc
- engine mounts
- VSS adapter
- crossmember spacers
- Porsche power-steering-pump bracket
- oil-pressure sender tee

Requires separately:
- C5 manual Corvette bellhousing
- LS flywheel / pressure plate
- remote oil filter

The exhaust packaging requires a compact 2014+ LT-family starter:
- **AC Delco 12691976** [S033][S034]

## Texas Performance Concepts

TPC hardware has a long documented 944 LS history and was still identified by builders as an available kit path in late 2024
[S019A]. Historical first-hand build documentation describes TPC mounts, bellhousing/clutch hardware, subframe spacers, header
choices, and brake solutions [S035].

During the September 2026 sweep the TPC web catalog was not reliably indexable, so this knowledge base should avoid presenting an
unverified current price or inventory state.

## Renegade Hybrids

Renegade remains active in 2026 as a Porsche LS conversion business and has extensive historical 944 LS experience. The public
site remains current, but the available indexed material is less explicit than G Force about exact 944 kit contents/pricing.

## Practical conclusion

For a builder purchasing today:
- G Force has the most transparent currently indexed 944 LS bill of materials.
- TPC and Renegade remain historically important and may still be viable suppliers, but current package contents should be
  verified directly before using old forum parts lists.
