# Nissan VK56 5.6L V8 — Feasibility Study

Status: **provisional feasibility-study engine**. A completed Porsche 944/VK56 installation has not been identified in the
baseline research.

## Why investigate it

The VK56DE is not obscure donor hardware. Nissan's factory specifications describe a longitudinal 5,552 cc DOHC
32-valve aluminum-block/aluminum-head V8 producing **317 hp and 385 lb-ft** in 2009 Titan form [S023].

Its strong naturally aspirated torque, aluminum construction and growing swap aftermarket make it exactly the type of
engine future 944 builders are likely to ask about.

## Initial packaging evidence

A builder physically measured a non-VVL VK56 at approximately [S024]:
- overall height, oil pan to top of intake: **33 in**
- overall length: **30 in**
- overall width: **30 in**
- Titan intake height: **10 in**
- top of intake to bottom of crank balancer: **25 in**
- bottom of balancer to bottom of oil pan: **8 in**

These are useful bounding-box measurements, not yet common-datum 944 engineering measurements.

## Current aftermarket evidence

C3 Performance currently sells an adapter for VK56DE/VK56VD truck/SUV engines to CD009 and later JK/VQ manual
transmissions. The adapter is based on 3D scan data and includes provisions for crank sensing, flywheel spacing and
starter mounting [S026].

This does **not** solve the Porsche torque-tube interface, but demonstrates that the engine now has a meaningful
manual-transmission swap ecosystem.

## Critical feasibility questions

1. Can the DOHC cylinder-head width fit between 944 rails, booster and steering shaft?
2. Can the ~33-inch reported height be reconciled with stock hood and crossmember/sump clearance?
3. Can a rear- or custom-sump arrangement work around the 944 crossmember and steering?
4. What is the true dressed and 944-ready mass?
5. Can a bellhousing/adapter be designed without excessive stack length?
6. Can A/C and power steering be retained?
7. Is the earlier port-injected VK56DE preferable to the later direct-injected VK56VD?
8. Does 385+ lb-ft create an unacceptable transaxle reliability burden?
9. Does the VK56 offer enough advantage over a compact aluminum LS to justify its much larger DOHC envelope?

## Current assessment

The VK56 belongs in this knowledge base even if the eventual conclusion is negative. A quantified explanation of
**why it does not fit** would be valuable to future builders and prevents the same feasibility work being repeated.
