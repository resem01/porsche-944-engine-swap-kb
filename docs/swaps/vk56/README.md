# Nissan VK56 5.6L V8 — Investigation Candidate

Status: **provisional**. The engine merits investigation but has not yet been demonstrated to be a practical Porsche 944 swap.

## Why investigate it

- 5,552 cc, 90-degree DOHC V8.
- Aluminum block and heads.
- Strong naturally aspirated torque and useful stock power.
- Growing contemporary swap interest.
- Commercial adapters exist for mating VK56DE/VK56VD variants to Nissan CD009/JK-series manual transmissions, demonstrating that the aftermarket is developing around the platform.

## Initial packaging evidence

A builder physically measured a non-VVL VK56 at approximately:

- overall height, oil pan to top of intake: **33 in**
- overall length, rear of block to front of intake: **30 in**
- overall width: **30 in**
- Titan intake height: **10 in**
- top of intake to bottom of crank balancer: **25 in**
- bottom of balancer to bottom of oil pan: **8 in**

These dimensions are useful screening data, but they are not yet referenced to the 944 project's preferred common datums. They must be re-measured or translated before direct comparison with the 944, 07K, LS and other candidates.

Source:
https://forums.hybridz.org/topic/133552-vk56-dimensions-build-question/

## Current swap-hardware evidence

C3 Performance currently offers VK56DE/VK56VD to Nissan VQ35/VQ37/VR30 manual-transmission adapter systems. This does **not** solve the Porsche torque-tube interface, but it shows an active adapter ecosystem and provides useful flywheel/clutch/starter design references.

Source:
https://www.c3adapters.com/product-page/vk50-56-to-vq35-37-6-speed-transmission-adapter-kit

## Engine-management considerations

The earlier VK56DE is attractive for investigation because it uses multi-point fuel injection and is materially simpler than the later direct-injected VK56VD. The VD adds direct injection and more sophisticated valve-control systems, increasing swap-control complexity.

Initial source:
https://www.enginelabs.com/news/nissan-vk56-engine-what-you-need-to-know/

## 944-specific evidence status

Initial searches have **not identified a completed Porsche 944/VK56 installation**. Until one is documented, this project should treat the installation as an engineering feasibility study rather than an established swap.

## Critical questions

1. Can the engine fit beneath the stock 944 hood while maintaining acceptable sump-to-crossmember clearance?
2. Can a practical sump/pickup arrangement clear the 944 steering/crossmember geometry?
3. How does the approximately 30-inch reported width interact with the brake booster, steering shaft and chassis rails?
4. What is the true dressed and 944-swap-ready mass?
5. Can an adapter/bellhousing be designed without creating excessive torque-tube input-shaft length or clutch-release complexity?
6. Can A/C and power steering be retained?
7. Which variant is preferable: early VK56DE, later CVTC VK56DE, or VK56VD?
8. What donor years and vehicles offer the best combination of cost, controls and accessories?
9. Does the resulting torque materially exceed the practical limits of commonly used 944/951 transaxles?
10. Does the engine provide enough advantage over an aluminum LS to justify the much greater packaging and fabrication burden?

## Promotion criterion

Promote the VK56 into the primary engine comparison only after enough evidence exists to define:
- credible physical fit
- swap-ready weight
- torque-tube adaptation path
- oiling solution
- engine-management path
- at least one viable installation architecture
