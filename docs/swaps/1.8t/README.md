# VW/Audi 1.8T 20V Swap

## Baseline status

The 1.8T is an established 944 conversion with active commercial support from Motor Werks Racing (MWR) [S012][S013].
It is therefore a serious comparison candidate rather than merely an older historical alternative to the 07K.

## Engine selection

MWR specifically supports the **058-base 1.8T**, found in its documented donor range in Audi A4 and Volkswagen Passat
applications. MWR states that the later 06A is not supported because the chain-driven oil pump increases the packaging
length [S013].

The conversion can use the factory AEB harness/ECU, but wiring modification is required and ECU tuning is strongly
recommended. It is not plug-and-play [S013].

## Chassis/drivetrain integration

MWR's installation hardware mates the 058 block to the naturally aspirated 944/924S bellhousing and torque tube.
For 944 Turbo and S2 chassis, MWR specifies use of the naturally aspirated 944 bellhousing and starter ring [S013].

The commercial hardware includes a billet adapter plate and custom flywheel, allowing retention of Porsche starter,
starter ring, clutch slave/fork and torque-tube architecture [S012].

## Packaging and practical limitations

MWR explicitly states:
- additional fabrication is required,
- the standard intake arrangement may require brake-booster deletion or custom intake/booster work,
- the conversion was developed primarily for track use,
- factory A/C and heat are not supported by their standard conversion,
- manual-transmission cars are the supported application [S013].

These limitations are important when comparing the 1.8T with the current 07K kit, which is marketed around preserving
more factory accessories.

## Weight evidence

S&P Automotive measured a fully dressed 1.8T + 02J, without A/C, at **445 lb**, and an 02J at **92 lb** [S010].
The resulting inferred engine-package weight is roughly **353 lb**, but this is not directly equivalent to 944 swap-ready trim.

MWR publishes a much lower "stage 1 engine" weight figure [S012]. Because the included components and measurement
configuration are not sufficiently comparable to the S&P data, this project will not mix the two values.

## Current maturity assessment

**Maturity: High.**

Strengths:
- current commercial adapter/mount/oiling/cooling hardware
- factory ECU can be retained
- broad tuning ecosystem
- compact four-cylinder layout
- documented 944 track-car history

Limitations:
- more fabrication than the current 07K kit
- brake-booster/intake conflict depending configuration
- no standard A/C/heat solution
- 058-specific donor requirement
