# 07K Cooling, Power Steering, and Air Conditioning

## Cooling-system packaging problem

The transverse 07K places important coolant connections at the rear of the engine. In a 944, that rear face is close to the firewall.

Early measurement in the development thread found roughly **60 mm / 2.5 in** between the rear coolant-flange mounting face and the firewall seam.

Source:
- [Page 59](https://rennlist.com/forums/944-turbo-and-turbo-s-forum/803341-vw-audi-07k-2-5l-20v-i5-swap-thread-59.html)

## Rear coolant flange

The production-oriented design relocated the factory-style ports to clear the firewall and added a dedicated Porsche coolant-temperature-sender location.

An early prototype was 3D printed in carbon-filled nylon.

Source:
- [Page 99](https://rennlist.com/forums/944-turbo-and-turbo-s-forum/803341-vw-audi-07k-2-5l-20v-i5-swap-thread-99.html)

The current Boost Brothers catalog sells a dedicated rear coolant flange as a separate component.

## Thermostat housing

The original transverse thermostat outlet points in an inconvenient direction for the longitudinal 944 installation.

The thread developed around:
- dedicated thermostat-housing/neck adaptation
- 1.8T thermostat-housing components
- routing that avoids a 180° hose turn near the firewall

Current Boost Brothers documentation requires:
- **1.8T thermostat housing cover 06A121121C**

Current source:
- [Boost Brothers full kit](https://www.boostbrothersgarage.com/products/944-07k-swap-full-kit)

## Documented cooling hose arrangement

A detailed 2020 post records a working routing scheme:

All cars:
- rear-flange large outlet → upper radiator
- lower radiator → thermostat neck
- rear-flange steam vent → expansion tank
- original thermostat-housing 3/4-in port capped

Useful documented hose/part examples:
- Dayco 72365 — rear engine toward upper radiator
- Dayco 72193 — lower radiator toward thermostat
- ICT Billet 1-1/4-in coupler where needed

Source:
- [Page 138](https://rennlist.com/forums/944-turbo-and-turbo-s-forum/803341-vw-audi-07k-2-5l-20v-i5-swap-thread-138.html)

## Heater and water-cooled turbo options

The same page documents four routing strategies:
1. no heat / no water-cooled turbo
2. heat / no water-cooled turbo
3. no heat / water-cooled turbo
4. heat / water-cooled turbo

Example parts include:
- GM 12380318 heater valve
- Dorman 47147 tee
- Dorman 56152 barb

These should be treated as one builder's proven configuration, not the only valid routing.

## Power steering

Most 07K donor cars did not use the same belt-driven hydraulic power-steering arrangement needed by the 944.

The conversion developed around:
- VW/Audi 1.8T hydraulic power-steering pump
- custom mounting bracket
- custom/dedicated pulley

Historical thread p/n:
- VW 1.8T pump **1J0422152G**

Current Boost Brothers full-kit page lists:
- 1.8T power-steering pump **964064** as the required pump reference
- dedicated relocation hardware/pulley

The current vendor listing should govern new builds; the older VW p/n remains useful for cross-reference.

## Why deleting power steering is not just removing the pump

The 07K accessory drive uses a dual-belt arrangement. The thread notes that the A/C compressor/pulley position participates in driving other accessories. The 944 conversion uses the power-steering relocation hardware partly to preserve that drive path.

A manual-rack build therefore still needs a valid pulley/accessory-drive solution.

## Air conditioning

A/C was explicitly **out of scope** in the 2019 base kit.

Why:
- stock 07K compressor does not fit the developed 944 layout
- exhaust/turbo occupies the most obvious alternate belt-drive area

Builders explored:
- reversed Honda compressor concepts
- custom exhaust-side compressor mounts
- electric A/C compressors

At least one builder fabricated an electric-compressor system retaining stock early-944 condenser/evaporator hardware, but the thread does not establish a standardized commercial A/C solution.
