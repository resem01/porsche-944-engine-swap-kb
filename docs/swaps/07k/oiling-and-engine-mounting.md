# 07K Oiling, Oil Pan, Engine Mounting, and Installation Geometry

## Oil-pan problem

The stock transverse 07K oil-pan system does not clear the Porsche 944 crossmember/rack geometry when the engine is installed longitudinally.

The development team considered:
- completely fabricated pan
- dry sump
- billet/machined pan
- modified factory structural upper casting

The selected production approach was to retain and machine the factory cast upper pan, then add a purpose-built lower rear sump.

## Development iterations

A second upper-pan attempt still sat closer to the crossmember than desired. The thread explicitly documents iterative machining/welding changes rather than a one-shot design.

A final production concept used:
- machined factory upper casting
- fabricated lower sump
- custom pickup arrangement
- dedicated brace/support

## Oil pickup

The stock 07K pickup was unsuitable for the deeper/repositioned sump.

The developed solution used:
- machined steel adapter at the 07K oil pump
- Audi/VW 1.8T-style pickup tube
- additional bracket tied into existing pan mounting points to reduce vibration

## Current oil-pan design

Boost Brothers currently describes the production pan as:
- machined stock upper pan/core
- baffled lower sump
- trap doors
- custom pickup
- factory crossmember clearance
- no chassis/body modification
- -10AN turbo oil-return provision

Current source:
- [Boost Brothers oil pan](https://www.boostbrothersgarage.com/products/944-07k-swap-oil-pan)

This current vendor configuration supersedes the early-development descriptions.

## Oil-filter block

The conversion architecture replaces/relocates the stock oil-filter arrangement with a compact block that supports:
- pressure sensing
- turbo-oil supply/return plumbing strategies
- chassis clearance

A documented early failure involved a **faulty check valve in an iAbed oil-filter block**, which caused inadequate oil supply to the cylinder head and lifter ticking. The developers later stated that turbo oil supply from the head was not the root cause.

## Engine mounts

The mount design evolved alongside:
- bellhousing position
- oil-pan clearance
- intake/hood clearance
- oil-filter-block packaging

By late 2018 the mount brackets had been revised to:
- work with the compact oil-filter block
- add reinforcement
- position the engine lower for hood clearance
- retain isolator bushings

Source:
- [Page 32](https://rennlist.com/forums/944-turbo-and-turbo-s-forum/803341-vw-audi-07k-2-5l-20v-i5-swap-thread-32.html)

## Crossmember strategy

A custom three-piece crossmember was explored during development to create additional oil-pan clearance and easier service access, but the commercial conversion ultimately evolved around retaining the **factory 944 crossmember**.

The current Boost Brothers kit explicitly states:
- stock crossmember retained
- stock power-steering rack retained
- no sheet-metal modification

Current source:
- [Boost Brothers full kit](https://www.boostbrothersgarage.com/products/944-07k-swap-full-kit)

## Installation lessons

A later developer retrospective offered three useful recommendations:

1. Remove the rear timing-chain cover while installing the upper oil pan.
2. Remove the crossmember while installing the engine.
3. Install the upper and lower oil pans before installing the engine.

Source:
- [Page 161](https://rennlist.com/forums/944-turbo-and-turbo-s-forum/803341-vw-audi-07k-2-5l-20v-i5-swap-thread-161.html)

## Chassis offset

The developers stated that early/late wheel offset does not materially change kit fitment.

## Status

**Mature subsystem, but installation procedure remains important.**

The current commercial pan/mount system is the result of several iterations. Earlier pan concepts and custom-crossmember ideas are historical, not current requirements.
