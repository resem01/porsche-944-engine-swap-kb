# 07K Bellhousing, Clutch, Pilot Bearing, and Torque-Tube Interface

This subsystem was one of the major engineering hurdles in the original development thread.

## Why a normal adapter plate was not enough

The 07K shares much of its bellhousing bolt pattern with the VW/Audi 1.8T, but the rear timing-chain housing occupies space that a normal longitudinal bellhousing or a 944 bellhousing wants to use.

Early investigation included:
- Porsche 931 bellhousing
- Audi 01E-pattern bellhousing
- modified stock 944 bellhousing
- simple adapter-plate concepts

The conclusion was that the 07K timing-chain housing made a purpose-built bellhousing the cleaner solution.

## Engine angle evolution

### Early concept — approximately 15°

Initial QuickTime development assumed roughly 15° engine inclination.

### Revised design — approximately 25°

The design was later changed to approximately **25° toward the passenger side**. The stated reason was to:
- clear the stock 944 hood
- maintain the factory Porsche crankshaft / torque-tube centerline height
- avoid lowering the torque tube and transaxle approximately 3/4 in
- avoid introducing unnecessary driveline angle or shifter-height changes

This is an important example of a superseded design decision that should remain documented.

## Final development-era clutch architecture

The bellhousing was designed around VW 02M-size clutch hardware:
- 240 mm flywheel and pressure plate architecture
- 944 Turbo 240 mm clutch disc / Porsche torque-tube spline
- reverse-mounted VW 02M starter
- custom pilot-bearing adapter because the Porsche torque-tube input shaft does not reach the native VW crank pilot location
- concentric GM hydraulic release bearing

A documented working 2019 configuration was:
- NineX / Boost Brothers bellhousing
- NineX pilot-bearing adapter
- SPEC SV81A 02M aluminum flywheel
- SPEC 02M pressure plate
- SPEC 944 Turbo Stage 3 clutch disc
- GM/Solstice hydraulic throwout bearing 24234308
- Kappadapt CHK101-2 TOB hydraulic adapter
- JEGS 635158 24-in -4AN hose
- Earl's AT9919BFEERL M12x1.0 to -4AN adapter
- stock Porsche 944 clutch master cylinder

## Release-bearing mounting hardware

Page 131 contains a useful installation detail:
- **M6 x 1.0 socket-head cap screws**
- **15-16 mm long**

The developer noted that these small bolts only retain the concentric slave in position; the bellhousing structure carries the clutch load.

Source:
- [Rennlist page 131](https://rennlist.com/forums/944-turbo-and-turbo-s-forum/803341-vw-audi-07k-2-5l-20v-i5-swap-thread-131.html)

## Bleeding the concentric slave

Two methods were reported to work:
1. power-bleed the reservoir and slowly cycle the clutch pedal
2. keep the reservoir full and slowly cycle the pedal through full stroke without a conventional slave bleeder

This should be considered builder procedure rather than a factory service method.

## Installation sequence notes

Before installing the engine, thread contributors recommended having installed:
- upper oil pan and pickup
- lower sump
- flywheel
- pilot-bearing adapter
- clutch disc
- bellhousing
- hydraulic TOB
- rear coolant flange

They recommended installing the exhaust manifold and power-steering hardware **after** the engine is in the chassis.

The thread also records difficulty getting the final inch or two of engagement when clutch alignment, subframe position, and sump/crossmember clearance were not ideal.

## Status

**Mature subsystem.**

The important historical lesson is not merely the final parts list: the purpose-built bellhousing exists because earlier adapter approaches conflicted with the 07K rear timing housing and because engine inclination/centerline geometry mattered.
