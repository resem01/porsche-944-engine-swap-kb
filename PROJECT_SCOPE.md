# Project Scope

## Objective

Create and maintain an open technical reference for credible Porsche 944/951 engine swaps,
combining historical development records with current parts availability, completed-build evidence,
physical measurements, and original engineering work.

## Phase 1 — Baselines and candidate comparison

Establish comparable data for:
- 1985 Porsche 944 NA
- Porsche 944 Turbo / 951
- Porsche 968 3.0L
- VW/Audi 07K
- VW/Audi 1.8T
- Honda K24 / K-series
- GM Ecotec
- aluminum GM LS
- Ford 2.3 EcoBoost

Comparison topics include:
- dressed and swap-ready weight
- engine dimensions using common datums
- stock and practical power/torque
- engine placement and center-of-mass implications
- torque-tube / bellhousing adaptation
- oil-pan and crossmember interference
- hood, firewall, brake-booster, radiator and accessory clearance
- cooling, fuel, electrical and ECU integration
- A/C and power-steering retention
- donor availability and cost
- kit completeness and current vendor support
- documented completed builds
- fabrication burden and unresolved issues

## Phase 2 — 07K development record

Extract substantive information from the long-running Rennlist 07K swap thread and linked
offshoot threads. Preserve:
- Boost Brothers kit development
- major design revisions
- measurements
- part numbers
- failures and lessons learned
- useful alternate approaches
- completed-builder experience

Discard:
- congratulations
- conversational repetition
- unsupported speculation with no continuing technical significance
- duplicate questions already resolved later in the thread

## Phase 3 — Original development

Add measurements, photographs, CAD designs, installation results and test data generated from
the project owner's 1985 944 and 1987 951 race-car chassis where appropriate.

## Publication model

GitHub is intended to be the canonical public, version-controlled publication.
ChatGPT Project/Work is the research and development workspace.

## Out of scope initially

A complete catalog of every engine ever fitted to a 944. Secondary engines remain discoverable
but do not receive full comparison treatment unless evidence justifies promotion.
